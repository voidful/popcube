Core Mechanics

1. Queue & Dock Scheduling

Deploy front cannons from 4 queues into 5 docks. Each cannon targets visible matching outer cubes on the active face and only frees its dock when its ammo hits zero.

2. Rotate & Peel Outer Shells

Drag to spin sculptures cleanly with 60-tooth Swiss watch gear detents, or use auto-turn when stalled. Open Queue Overview anytime to pause and inspect hidden layers.

3. 17 Chapters & Taiwan Tour

Progress from classic radios and lighthouses (Ch. 1–5) into 12 Taiwanese cultural chapters: Xiaolongbao, Bubble Tea, Tofu Pudding, Shaved Ice, Taipei Metro, and Yushan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play PopCube?

Tap the front cannon of any of four queues to deploy it into one of five dock slots. Mounted cannons fire automatically at visible outer voxels of their matching color and vacate the slot once out of ammo. Drag the 3D sculpture to expose hidden faces or use Queue Overview to plan ahead.

Can PopCube be completed offline without paying or watching ads?

Yes. All 100 levels and daily remixed challenges are mathematically verified to be 3-star clearable using 5 slots with zero boosters or purchases. No account or internet connection is required for single-player levels.

How do I restore my Studio Pass on a new device?

Open Studio Settings in PopCube and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID. Consumable coins and rescue tickets are stored locally on the device.
